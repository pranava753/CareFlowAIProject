# Dermatology Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for Dermatology. It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
Dermatology is staffed by Dr. Reynolds, Dr. Alvarez at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 20 minutes
- Referral validity window: 120 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 30 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 12 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Referral letter

## Preparation instructions
Avoid applying makeup or lotion to the affected area on the day of the visit. Bring a list of current skincare products.

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- Skin Biopsy, punch (CPT-11104): estimated cost $210
- Cryotherapy, lesion removal (CPT-17110): estimated cost $150
- Full-body Skin Exam (CPT-99203): estimated cost $180

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for Dermatology specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
