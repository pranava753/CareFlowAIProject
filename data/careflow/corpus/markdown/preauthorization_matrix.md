# Pre-Authorization Matrix

## Purpose
This matrix states, for each plan and specialty, whether the specialty's typical procedure requires pre-authorization, the expected turnaround time, and the estimated cost used for pre-authorization review. These figures are generated from the same plan data as the Cost-Share Schedule and must not be restated with different numbers elsewhere.

## Pre-authorization thresholds by plan tier
- Silver Access 100 (PLN-SILVER-100, Silver tier): procedures estimated at or above $400 require pre-authorization; standard turnaround is 7 business days.
- Gold Complete 200 (PLN-GOLD-200, Gold tier): procedures estimated at or above $700 require pre-authorization; standard turnaround is 5 business days.
- Platinum Priority 300 (PLN-PLATINUM-300, Platinum tier): procedures estimated at or above $1000 require pre-authorization; standard turnaround is 3 business days.

## Escalation
A pre-authorization request that receives no payer response within the turnaround window above must be escalated to a human care coordinator; the assistant must not tell a patient a procedure is approved unless the eligibility system shows an "approved" status.
