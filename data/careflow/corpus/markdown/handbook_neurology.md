# Neurology Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for Neurology. It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
Neurology is staffed by Dr. Mora, Dr. Brown at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 45 minutes
- Referral validity window: 90 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 30 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 24 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Referral letter
- Prior imaging/EEG reports (if any)

## Preparation instructions
Bring a written timeline of symptom onset and any prior neuroimaging reports. Continue current medications unless told otherwise.

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- EEG, routine (CPT-95816): estimated cost $420
- MRI, Brain without contrast (CPT-70551): estimated cost $1250
- Nerve Conduction Study (CPT-95909): estimated cost $340

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for Neurology specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
