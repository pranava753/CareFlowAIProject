# Endocrinology Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for Endocrinology. It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
Endocrinology is staffed by Dr. Hall, Dr. Santiago at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 30 minutes
- Referral validity window: 90 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 42 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 24 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Referral letter
- Recent lab reports

## Preparation instructions
Fast for 8 hours before your visit if bloodwork is expected; water is fine. Bring your most recent lab results.

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- HbA1c Panel (CPT-83036): estimated cost $60
- Thyroid Ultrasound (CPT-76536): estimated cost $280
- Bone Density Scan (DEXA) (CPT-77080): estimated cost $220

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for Endocrinology specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
