# Cardiology Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for Cardiology. It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
Cardiology is staffed by Dr. Johnson, Dr. Carrillo at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 40 minutes
- Referral validity window: 90 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 14 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 24 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Referral letter
- Prior cardiac test reports (if any)

## Preparation instructions
Bring a list of current medications and any prior ECG or echocardiogram reports. Avoid caffeine for 4 hours before a stress test.

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- Resting ECG (CPT-93000): estimated cost $120
- Stress Echocardiogram (CPT-93351): estimated cost $950
- Holter Monitor (24h) (CPT-93224): estimated cost $380

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for Cardiology specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
