# Orthopedics Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for Orthopedics. It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
Orthopedics is staffed by Dr. Ward, Dr. Brown at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 30 minutes
- Referral validity window: 60 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 21 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 24 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Referral letter
- Prior imaging reports (if any)

## Preparation instructions
Wear loose clothing that allows access to the affected joint. Bring any prior X-ray or MRI films on disc or portal link.

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- Joint X-ray (2-view) (CPT-73562): estimated cost $140
- MRI, Knee without contrast (CPT-73721): estimated cost $1100
- Corticosteroid Joint Injection (CPT-20610): estimated cost $260

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for Orthopedics specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
