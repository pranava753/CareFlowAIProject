# Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT). It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) is staffed by Dr. Harrison, Dr. Farley at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 25 minutes
- Referral validity window: 90 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 21 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 24 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Referral letter
- Prior audiogram (if any)

## Preparation instructions
Bring any prior hearing test results. If nasal endoscopy is expected, avoid a heavy meal in the 2 hours before your visit.

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- Diagnostic Nasal Endoscopy (CPT-31231): estimated cost $300
- Audiometry, comprehensive (CPT-92557): estimated cost $160
- Tympanometry (CPT-92567): estimated cost $90

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
