# General Medicine Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for General Medicine. It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
General Medicine is staffed by Dr. Lynch, Dr. Thomas at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 20 minutes
- Referral validity window: 180 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 30 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 12 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card

## Preparation instructions
Bring your current medication list and any home-monitoring logs (e.g. blood pressure, glucose).

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- Annual Wellness Visit (CPT-99397): estimated cost $150
- Basic Metabolic Panel (CPT-80048): estimated cost $45
- Vaccination Administration (CPT-90471): estimated cost $35

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for General Medicine specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
