# Gastroenterology Service-Line Handbook

## Overview
This handbook covers clinic operations for Gastroenterology. It describes appointment logistics, preparation, and coverage information for this service line only -- it does not contain diagnostic or treatment guidance.

## Providers
Gastroenterology is staffed by Dr. Snow, Dr. Clements at this clinic chain.

## Appointment logistics
- Standard appointment length: 30 minutes
- Referral validity window: 60 days from issue date
- Recommended follow-up scheduling window: within 14 days of the prior visit
- Cancellation notice required: 48 hours

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Referral letter
- Current medication list

## Preparation instructions
If a procedure is scheduled you will receive separate fasting/bowel-prep instructions by SMS 48 hours in advance.

## Common procedures and estimated cost
- Upper GI Endoscopy (CPT-43235): estimated cost $1350
- Colonoscopy, screening (CPT-45378): estimated cost $1600
- H. pylori Breath Test (CPT-83013): estimated cost $130

## Coverage
Whether these procedures require pre-authorization, and the expected turnaround time, depends on the patient's plan -- see the Pre-Authorization Matrix for Gastroenterology specifically. Do not assume coverage terms from another specialty's handbook apply here; figures are specialty-specific.
